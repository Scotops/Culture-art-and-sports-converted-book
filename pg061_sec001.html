Participating in various games

Let us stretch our bodies.

You can stretch:

1.

While standing;

2.

While lying down;

3.

While sitting;

4.

While bending your legs; and

5.

While leaning forward.

How to stretch while standing

1.

Stand upright and spread your legs.

2.

Lift up your left arm.

3.

Hold your waist with your right hand.

4.

Lean to the right.

5.

Switch hands.
