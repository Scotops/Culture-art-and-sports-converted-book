Activity 12

Skip rope with two legs.

Skipping with one leg (single player)

How to skip with one leg

1.

Hold one end of the rope with one hand.

2.

Hold the other end of the rope with the other hand.

3.

Swing the rope upwards and forwards.

4.

When the rope touches the ground, jump without touching it.

5.

Start jumping with one foot.

6.

Keep jumping while counting.

7.

If you touch the rope, let your friend take a turn.

8.

The one who jumps the most without touching the rope wins.
